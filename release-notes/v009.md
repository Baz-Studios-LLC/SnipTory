# SnipTory v009

- Hide each History card's trash button until the card is hovered. The button stays visible while the pointer is over the button itself. Deletion confirmation and the Delete keyboard shortcut remain available.
- Consolidate the gallery header into one compact toolbar: History and capture count on the left, matching-height Clear history and Capture buttons on the right. Remove the tagline and duplicate section heading to give the cards more room.

Validated with a release build. Interactive testing is left to the user.

Update through **Options → Check for updates**. New installations can use **SnipTory-v009-Setup.exe**. Binaries are currently unsigned.
